A shape drifts toward a target zone—press at the exact right instant and you score big; a beat early or late and you don't. Alignee is a game about precision, not luck. It's simple to learn, but difficult to master.

## Why play Alignee?

- **Play your way:** Use the volume button or switch to tap-to-lock in Settings—you choose your input method
- **Built for focus:** Clean, distraction-free Liquid Glass UI optimized for quick play, with no pay-to-win mechanics
- **Quick sessions:** 30–90 second rounds perfect for waiting in line, on your commute, or whenever you want to test your reflexes
- **Play offline:** No connection required for gameplay—Game Center and iCloud sync happen whenever you're online

## Getting started

1. Download <a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/alignee/id6759492125" style={{ textDecoration: 'underline' }}>Alignee</a> from the App Store.
2. Choose a volume button or tap-to-lock as your input method in Settings.
3. Press play and lock the shape at the perfect moment to score.
4. Track your stats, complete the daily challenge, and unlock achievements in Game Center.

### Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I lock the shape?**

**A:** Press either volume button at the moment you want to lock the shape in place. If you'd rather not use the volume button, enable tap-to-lock in Settings to use the screen instead.

**Q: What's the daily challenge?**

**A:** Every player gets the same level each day, generated from a shared, UTC-based seed. You get one attempt with no retries, so make it count—a color-coded calendar tracks your completions and streaks.

**Q: Can I remove ads?**

**A:** Yes. Purchase the one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase to eliminate banner and interstitial ads. Optional reward ads for retries remain available if you want them.

**Q: Does Alignee work offline?**

**A:** Yes, you can play offline anytime. An internet connection is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and iCloud sync.
