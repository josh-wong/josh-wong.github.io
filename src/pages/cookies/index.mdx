# Cookie policy

This website uses cookies and third-party services. Optional analytics don't run until you accept them in the cookie banner.

## Analytics

If you accept analytics, the website loads Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager to help improve the site. Algolia search insights can also collect search interaction data when you use search.

If you decline, the website doesn't load these analytics. A cookie saves your choice for 150 days. You can change your choice at any time using the button below. Reload other open tabs for the change to take effect.

<CookiePreferences />

Changing your choice doesn't delete cookies already stored by third parties. You can remove those cookies through your browser settings.

## Other services

The website uses browser storage for features such as notification read status, theme preferences, and offline access.

Algolia processes your search queries when you use site search. The status badge connects to the status service, the video on the About page uses YouTube, and some images and code examples load from GitHub. These services may receive information such as your IP address independently of your analytics choice.
